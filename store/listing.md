# The Praying App

## Store listing copy

- **App name** — The Praying App
- **Subtitle** — Your personal prayer book
- **Short description** — Keep prayer requests, remember prayer time, and look back on answered prayers.

Write down what’s on your heart. Give each request a category, return to it when you pray, and record the day it was answered.

Open Today to find a prayer from your list and see which days you prayed this week. Set a daily reminder for a time that fits your routine. The notification keeps your prayer text private.

Use your prayer book offline as a guest. If you have a prayer portal account, sign in with your email and password to sync requests, categories, and prayer history. You choose whether to copy your guest prayers into your account.

## Publisher handoff

The publisher supplies the support and privacy-policy URLs, store account details, reviewer login and final privacy declarations. Guest books are saved on the device. When a person signs in, their account identifier, session, requests, categories, answers, and prayer history are processed through the configured Supabase project. Notifications are scheduled locally. Consider both the portal's data practices and the information people may enter in prayer requests when completing store disclosures.
